This is the official repository for my scenario "The Burning Rain" that is on the Steam workshop, it can be found here:

<a href="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1732659749">The Burning Rain</a>

All wars are over, the last world war took place across the globe in a desperate attempt to stop the White Plague, ending in vain, and now the world lies in ruin.

15 years ago the bombs fell, they tried to control the outbreak of the White Plague with nuclear weaponry, chemical agents, and even mind-control experiments and tests.

But all their tests, weaponry, and main goals were defeated and as a result of mass destructions, land poisoned with chemical agents, there are 'dead' walking among the living; Zombies.

After all these events, stripped of mind and reason, leaving only an empty, mindless shell. The brainless bodies then turn into zombies, following only the most basic instincts, wandering aimlessly around the land without weapons or even clothing. Some zombies are little more than sluggish walking corpses, caring only about food and sleep, some can run even. They eat and drink just about anything they can get their hands on. Usually, they roam the land without any apparent purpose. However, as soon as they sense the presence of a human being, they attack immediately.

Experienced travelers have learned to avoid them if they can.

The zombies are somewhat all that roams the lands of Chernarus, there really isn't much else to see.

Some wandering survivors speak of other creatures, that some believe to be folk stories from Slavic mythology, or perhaps just stories to frighten other survivor groups from wandering the wastes.

Nevertheless, you are one of the few that survived. This is YOUR story.

Disclaimer:

This scenario is recommended for hardcore players only. It is extremely difficult and you will face many threats to your survival. You need to scavenge for food, water, and medicine in a post-nuclear war Chernarus. You can find some valuable items in military zones, but beware of the dangers. Some enemies or situations in the scenario are fatal and impossible to escape. If you die, you lose everything, but your world progress is saved. This scenario mimics a multiplayer game mode, but you play alone. If you are ready for this challenge, we hope you enjoy it!
